PINAKA-

Pinaka- is a prefix used to form the superlative of an adjective

mahaba → long
PINAKAmahaba → the longest

-NAKAKA-

Nakaka- is a prefix used to signify that something is causing or producing something else

takot → fear
NAKAKAtakot → causing fear → scary

-PAG-

Pag- is a prefix used to either signify that something is happening upon the action of the root

dating → arrival
PAGdating → upon arriving

or to form the gerund of a verb

isip → thought
PAG-iisip → thinking

-NGITNGIT-

ngit-ngit is an expression that describes the act of gritting your teeth and is thus used to convey restrained frustration or anger

In tagalog it is possible to increase the intensity of any given word by simply repeating it

ngitngit → frustrated
ngitngit-ngitngit → extremely annoyed

-PAGSISINUNGA-

pagsisinungalin is a noun that means lying/to tell a lie

pag- → gerund (telling)
-si- → conjugations in the future or present require doubling a syllable
-sinungaling → liar

-SINUNGALING-

Sinungaling is the tagalog noun for liar it is also a root used for words relating to lies, the act of lying, or being a liar

mag- → prefix signifying an actor-focused verb
magSINUNGALING → to lie

-AN

-an is a suffix that functions as an object trigger. In other words it does/makes/causes something to a person or thing (object)


extremely frustrating game of lies
